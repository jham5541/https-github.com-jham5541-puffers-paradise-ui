# Combat and the Heat System

## Combat

- Third-person aiming
- Melee combat
- Cover system
- Weapon classes
- Armor
- Health and stamina
- Injuries
- Revive system
- Nonlethal weapons
- Vehicle combat
- Group tactics

Built on the **Gameplay Ability System** (abilities, attributes, gameplay
effects for injuries/armor/stamina). UI presentation must be original — do not
copy GTA's weapon wheel or HUD trade dress.

## Heat system (original law-enforcement response)

Six escalation tiers instead of star ratings:

| Tier | Name | Response |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Suspicion | NPCs report; patrols look for you |
| 2 | Detainment | Officers attempt a stop and arrest |
| 3 | Active pursuit | Chase units, spike strips |
| 4 | Tactical response | Armed tactical teams, roadblocks |
| 5 | Regional lockdown | District checkpoints, air support |
| 6 | Federal response | Overwhelming force, safehouses raided |

## Factors that affect response

Witnesses, cameras, masks, license plates, weapon type, district, police
presence, organization reputation, prior crimes, and per-server rules.

Heat decays over time, faster when unseen; masks and plate swaps reduce
identification, not the crime itself. All heat state is server-authoritative.
