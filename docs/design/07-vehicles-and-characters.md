# Vehicles and Character Creation

## Vehicles

Classes: cars, motorcycles, trucks, buses, boats, helicopters, aircraft,
construction vehicles, emergency vehicles, commercial vehicles.

Player actions: purchase, lease, rent, insure, modify, repair, repossess, sell,
store, register, track, share access.

Driving is built on **Chaos Vehicles**. All designs are **original vehicles
from fictional manufacturers** — no real marques, no near-clone trade dress.

## Character creation

Customizable: face, body, height range, hair, clothing, voice, walk style,
tattoos, accessories, background, skills, personality traits, profession.

No celebrity likenesses without permission; the creator should make it hard to
reproduce a specific real person deliberately.
