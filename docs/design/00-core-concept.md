# Core Concept

**Game:** The Puffers Club — working title *Empire City Online*

A persistent 3D open-world crime, business, social, and property-building game.

## Pillars

1. **A living, player-shaped city.** Districts change hands, buildings rise,
   and neighborhoods develop because players built them — the world is partly
   player-built and player-operated instead of fixed.
2. **Crime and commerce are the same ladder.** Legal and illegal businesses use
   the same underlying systems (property, staff, inventory, customers, risk).
3. **Organizations are first-class.** Teams, clubs, gangs, companies, and
   political groups have real structure: ranks, permissions, shared assets,
   territory, and income.
4. **Player-run servers.** Controlled server templates let communities define
   their own rules, economies, and roleplay cultures.

## The fantasy

The player arrives in a city undergoing rapid redevelopment. Corporations,
organized crime groups, political leaders, unions, property developers, and
neighborhood organizations fight for control. The player builds influence,
chooses allies, creates businesses, and determines which groups control which
districts.

## What players do

- Cinematic main missions; side missions; dynamic world events
- Form and run organizations of every type
- Buy, rent, design, construct, and manage buildings (most are enterable)
- Operate legal and fictionalized illegal businesses
- Host private or public servers with custom rules and economies
- Trade assets with other players
- Fight, race, socialize, work, invest, and govern

## Non-goals (at least at launch)

- Country-sized maps. We ship **one dense city**, starting with one district.
- Free-form geometry building. Construction is **modular and validated**.
- Thousands of players in one seamless shard. We start at 32, grow to 64,
  target 100–128 after optimization.
- Pay-to-win monetization. Cosmetics, server subscriptions, building themes,
  optional content packs, and convenience only.
