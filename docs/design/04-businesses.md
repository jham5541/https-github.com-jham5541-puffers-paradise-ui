# Business Systems

Businesses create **real gameplay**, not passive menus.

## Legal businesses

Restaurants, clothing stores, vehicle dealerships, construction companies,
security companies, nightclubs, taxi companies, delivery companies, music
studios, real-estate firms, hotels, repair shops, logistics companies, event
venues, media companies.

## Illegal businesses (fictionalized, gameplay-oriented)

Contraband distribution, vehicle theft rings, underground gambling, smuggling,
forgery, black-market trading, illegal racing, protection operations, hacking
contracts, counterfeit goods.

All illegal-business gameplay is abstract and mechanical — risk/reward loops,
heat, and law-enforcement pressure — never instructional about real crime.

## The business gameplay loop

1. Acquire a property.
2. Register an organization.
3. Obtain licenses.
4. Purchase equipment.
5. Hire players or NPC workers.
6. Source inventory.
7. Set prices.
8. Advertise.
9. Serve customers.
10. Manage risk, theft, competition, and law enforcement.
11. Expand to additional locations.
12. Sell, franchise, or merge the business.

## MVP businesses (four, fully playable)

| Business | Loop it proves |
|---|---|
| Taxi company | Player jobs, dispatch, vehicle fleets |
| Convenience store | Inventory, NPC customers, pricing |
| Nightclub | Social spaces, events, door security, ambience |
| Construction company | Bridge into the building system |
