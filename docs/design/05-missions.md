# Missions

## Main story

Cinematic but **original**. Premise: the player arrives in a city undergoing
rapid redevelopment. Corporations, organized crime groups, political leaders,
unions, property developers, and neighborhood organizations fight for control.
The player builds influence, chooses allies, creates businesses, and determines
which groups control which districts.

Main mission types: robberies, escapes, investigations, infiltration, corporate
espionage, protection, political manipulation, property acquisition, street
racing, rescue missions, construction disputes, business sabotage, territory
conflicts.

## Side missions

Taxi work, delivery jobs, security work, bounty contracts, vehicle recovery,
street races, property inspections, photography, courier work, repair jobs,
construction contracts, club promotion, talent management, emergency response,
detective work, real-estate tasks.

## Dynamic missions

Generated from economy and world state. Examples:

- A business needs an urgent delivery.
- A player misses a loan payment.
- A district loses electricity.
- A club requires security.
- A warehouse is attacked.
- A landlord requests repairs.
- A faction places a bounty.
- A city event creates high transportation demand.

## Player-created missions (post-launch)

A mission editor where players define: title, description, start location,
required players, enemy types, vehicles, objectives, dialogue, timers, rewards,
failure conditions, cutscene cameras, interior locations, difficulty,
recommended equipment.

Every published mission must pass: automated profanity screening, copyright
screening, geometry validation, reward-economy validation, exploit checks —
plus manual reporting tools.

## MVP scope

- **8 main missions** (one complete arc in the launch district)
- **20 side missions** across the categories above
