# Step gameplay: every mission objective plays

Every objective in `Content/Data/Missions/*.json` now plays as real gameplay, chosen from the
objective's Type and description. No mission JSON was changed. The mission runtime
(`UPuffersMissionSubsystem`) still owns progress, stages, choices, checkpoints, fail events and rewards.
The new step system only spawns gameplay for the objectives that are active right now. When a step is
won, it reports progress through `ReportObjectiveProgress`.

Coverage: 99 missions, 597 objectives, 0 on the stand-in prop. See `Docs/StepCoverage.md`, which is
generated by `python3 Scripts/step_coverage.py`. BL01 is excluded because it keeps its own level
(L_PuffingtonPen).

## How an objective picks its gameplay

1. Start from the defaults for its Type in StepRules.json (for example, TALK plays as Conversation and ESCAPE as Escape).
2. Apply the first keyword rule that matches, reading top to bottom. Keywords match at the start of a word, so "chase" doesn't match "purchase". `"Choice": true` limits a rule to choice-group options. Most INTERACT and BUSINESS_DECISION choice options become one decision menu that shows the cash and the flag each option sets. Action options listed above that rule keep their action: "Tackle the teen" plays as a Chase, "Follow the guy" as a Tail, and "Draw and fire" as Combat.
3. Apply the objective's override, if it has one.

To change how an objective plays, edit StepRules.json and rerun the coverage script. You don't need to rebuild.

## Controls (in addition to the existing ones)

| Key | Use |
|---|---|
| E | Start a step at its marker, interact, tackle, flag a threat, talk a troublemaker down |
| Z | Signature ability: Mac Vision, Trendsetter, Wrong Turn, Talk That Shit |
| P | Raise or lower the phone camera (Photo and Video). LMB takes the shot |
| RMB / LMB | Aim / fire (Combat) |
| 1-4 or arrow keys | Conversation options, Rhythm beats |
| Space | Timing bar |
| F or Shift | Shove (Defend), sprint (Chase) |
| K | Switch character when a Switch step asks |
| Backspace | Leave a conversation or menu (no penalty) |
| C | Crouch (Stealth, Combat cover) |

## M01 "Mall Cop" walkthrough (the proof mission)

1. **Briefing** (Conversation). Talk to the supervisor and pick a tone. Any tone finishes it, and the tone changes his reply.
2. **Check the camera wall** ×3 (Memory). Watch a sequence on the wall and repeat it.
3. **Mac Vision sweep** (Scan). Mac Vision is lent for this step. Press Z, and threats glow red. Look at one and press E. Three false alarms fails the step.
4. **Choice: Threat**. Two markers appear, and only one can be started.
   - Tackle the teen (Chase). Sprint after him and tackle with E or F. Sets Mac_Overreacted.
   - Follow the tag-switcher (Tail). Stay in range without raising suspicion, and stop when he looks back. Sets Mac_GoodInstinct.
5. Depending on the choice:
   - Apologize to his mom (Persuade). Read her and pick an approach.
   - Detain the tag-switcher (Approach). Close in without him noticing, then press E.
6. **File the incident report** (Memory).

Choices carry forward. In M02 the supervisor and Dre react to which threat you chose, and the
difficulty shifts up or down a little.

## What's still placeholder

- NPCs are the existing capsule NPCs with name tags, not MetaHumans. There are no animations, VFX or audio.
- Dialogue lines are generic templates built around the objective text and speaker name. Real written lines, voice and cutscenes are not part of this work.
- Step NPCs and markers spawn around the objective's prop, NPC or volume. If there's none, they spawn 7 m in front of the player. They don't use real map locations yet.
- Shots, view cones and paths are drawn as debug lines. The overlay is a debug-draw panel, not UMG.
- Vehicles: EnterVehicle spawns a garage car. Vehicle chases use the existing vehicle class.
- Tuning is first-pass, with difficulty around 0.4. Expect to adjust timers and detection after playtesting.
